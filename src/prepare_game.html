Which files can I use?

  • Disc image: select an .iso, or a .cue/.toc together with all its .bin files.
  • Already prepared: select your .ass files below. No conversion is needed.
Optional: update the full game to version 3.0

Patch 3.0 adds extra cars and includes the earlier game-data updates. Older patches are not needed.

Patch 3.1 only fixes the original Windows executable; it adds no game data for OpenMH.

If you have motorp30.exe, select it together with your full-game files. The update will be included in your game package.

Otherwise, leave No update selected. You can play without it.

Prepare on Windows

  1. Download OpenMH and extract the ZIP.
  2. Insert your Motorhead CD and open a terminal in that folder.
  3. Run this command, replacing D: with your CD drive:
.\openmotorex.exe --compact D: game.ass

For a disc image, replace D: with its CUE, TOC or ISO path. Keep its BIN files alongside it. To prepare patch 3.0 separately:

.\openmotorex.exe motorp30.exe patch30.ass

Choose In your browser above and add game.ass and, if prepared, patch30.ass.

Prepare on Linux

  1. Download OpenMH and unpack it.
  2. Insert your Motorhead CD and open a terminal in that folder.
  3. Run this command, replacing /dev/sr0 if your drive uses another path:
./openmotorex --compact /dev/sr0 game.ass

For a disc image, use its CUE, TOC or ISO path instead. Keep its BIN files alongside it. To prepare patch 3.0 separately:

./openmotorex motorp30.exe patch30.ass

Choose In your browser above and add game.ass and, if prepared, patch30.ass.
